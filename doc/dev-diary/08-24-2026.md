## [erlbutt](https://github.com/cmoid/erlbutt)

Mostly this past week I worked on finding my brain. Unlike many others I suffer horribly from jet lag and take a full week to begin to feel normal. I think this year my main running route will be this [long Reef headland](https://sydneyuncovered.com/long-reef-headland/). Two laps gets me my daily 5 miles. It's hard running along ocean and watching whales, but someone has to do it.

I did do some work on erlbutt, but mostly just reading the literature about `P2P`. I think trust is almost a third very hard problem along with bad actors and discovery. The three are very much related, perhaps there's a CAP theorem hiding. 

With respect to *feed manifests*, or maybe we should call them *feed glimpses*, the big question for me is whether they should be *author-curated* or *peer-attested*. The latter seems much more complex and gets to the heart of trust and how it's propagated. There's a big difference between trusting an institution and trusting a peer. I can declare that I trust my bank or my hospital or a college, but when I declare I trust Sally more than Bob then that's a can of worms in the social context.

Where this comes up is in both deciding whether a *feed glimpse* warrants pulling the entire feed and also whether to fetch archives of feeds to fully validate.


## [silkpurse](https://github.com/cmoid/silkpurse)

I did spend some time this past week putting together a packaged [release](https://github.com/cmoid/silkpurse/releases/tag/v0.1.0) for Silkpurse. I'm going to start publishing relases of this theseus branch I've been developing. The primary goal is to have a patchwork like client that I can use to suss out these new features in erlbutt that possibly will extend the protocol. Note that this is OSX only and ARM64. It's pretty easy to build other platforms or just run the source, especially for nodejs hackers. Think of it as a stripped down version of Patchwork. It's stable, and to me speedier, but I haven't measured that. I regularly switch back and forth between silkpurse and poncho with no problem, but *NOTE* that it uses `~/.silkpurse` by default. You can change this easily (see the docs) but please take care about `~/.ssb` and know that dance.
